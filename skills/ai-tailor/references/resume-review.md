# Resume review and technical optimization

This guide is part of ai-tailor. Use the selected BASE, complete JD, current candidate context and the evidence ledger. No external review/optimization skill is needed. Preserve the approved two-page Calibri design and confirmed facts; job-specific emphasis changes do not authorize new claims.

## Initial review

1. Detect experience level from actual full-time chronology (Sanjeev: 3+ years), and target role/market from the JD. Keep internship/project experience separate. Evaluate frontend-focused scope and current preferences before editing.
2. Read extracted text and inspect both pages. Record concrete strengths and weaknesses in parsing/reading order, section headings, contact links, role/date associations, technical relevance, impact, density and readability. A local extractor result is not an employer ATS result.
3. Compare mandatory/preferred JD requirements with demonstrated, context-supported, adjacent, skills-only and unsupported evidence. List present, buried and missing phrases, with source/evidence anchors. Do not infer a missing skill merely from unfamiliar wording or claim exact proficiency from adjacent experience.
4. Record the seven-sins diagnostic with ✓ = observed issue, ✗ = no observed issue: unstructured tool lists; vague outcomes without evidence; passive responsibility-only bullets/pronouns; generic summary; page bloat; missing relevant projects; supported JD language omitted/buried. Qualitative outcomes are valid—never invent metrics to clear a diagnostic. The approved BASE’s three projects remain required.
5. Prioritize concrete fixes and quote before/after wording with the JD reason and evidence. Distinguish proposed edits from changes actually made. Preserve effective wording. Do not infer rejection causes or hiring outcomes from this review.

If a score is useful, label it a heuristic: parseability 25, supported JD coverage 25, impact/evidence 20, structure/readability 15, contact/link completeness 15. Explain each score; no score, coverage percentage or reviewer verdict is an acceptance gate. Keep search fitness ranking separate. Report unsupported requirements as gaps regardless of score.

## Optimize after building the evidence ledger

- Reorder relevant skills within clear categories; keep exact supported terminology and expand abbreviations when useful. Do not add unverified tools, versions or proficiency.
- Within each original role, surface the most relevant engineering mechanisms and results first. Preserve dates, employer, attribution, scale, maturity and metrics. Never move an achievement into another role to improve apparent fit.
- Improve weak bullets with concrete action, implementation and supported outcome. Prefer natural concise language over repetitive templates. Keep transferable experience distinct from direct proficiency.
- Tune the existing summary to the target scope using supported experience; retain 3+ years full-time. Keep frontend, backend/data and AI/tooling evidence where relevant, without turning projects into employment.
- Preserve the three approved projects and their links. Compress repetition within the original role/project; log distinct achievements omitted and why. Skills-only mentions do not substitute for hands-on evidence.
- Record the independently planned complete final wording before PDF verification. Use the direct-PDF procedure for edits, all-page visual review, extraction, font/link checks and protected-input checks.

Write results into the analysis.md sections specified by ai-tailor. Never claim an unperformed review or test passed.
